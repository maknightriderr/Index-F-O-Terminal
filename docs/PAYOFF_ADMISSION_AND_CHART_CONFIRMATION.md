# Payoff admission and chart confirmation

New paper entries use RISK-3.0 and `+payoff-admission.1`, replacing the live display-only admission stamp. History and open trade plans are not rewritten. This is a constraint on new trade geometry, not evidence that accepted strategies are profitable.

All five live option-builder paths explicitly enable final payoff admission. They retain the existing target model and stop geometry, then require unrounded `(target - entry - estimated round-trip cost) / (entry - stop + estimated round-trip cost)` of at least the existing 1.5 floor (2.0 when the rich-IV rule applies). A stop whose structural invalidation lies beyond the 45% premium ceiling is refused rather than clipped and admitted. The new path always preserves structural/base stop construction rather than invoking legacy target-based stop squeezing. Refused plans remain visible as blocked research evidence; an `rrPlan` cannot clear the refusal. No reward target is increased to pass. Existing open trades retain their exit and trailing rules. Opt-out analytics calls retain old outputs for historical reproduction.

These thresholds were already defined in the application; they are not newly optimized on historical P&L and are not proven optimal. The change can substantially reduce trade counts. Forward results must be compared by exact risk/logic version, source and intraday/positional mode, including expired outcomes and costs. Do not present this admission filter as a proven improvement in expectancy.

Chart pattern and reversal analysis now excludes forming bars. A swing needing three right-hand bars is timestamped at its confirmation candle, not at its earlier origin. The pattern label says candidate and shape fit rather than presenting the heuristic score as a probability. Intraday OB formation and three-candle FVG formation cannot span missing/overnight bars; daily candles still allow non-trading calendar gaps. Detector definitions remain heuristics, not exchange facts or verified predictive signals. OB/FVG chart tests and fixture screenshots do not establish profitability or validate any unobserved user's chart.

Validation includes unchanged legacy golden/regression outputs, weak-payoff refusals, qualifying geometry unchanged, structural clipping refusal, rich-IV threshold, selected-expiry repricing with acceptance and refusal, and a full live/taped replay that now refuses the formerly admitted 0.04 net-R:R fixture without minting a signal. Chart tests cover exact close time, future-bar confirmation, missing-candle gaps and zone lifecycle. Browser checks cover the fixture chart, zone toggles, dark/light, mobile overflow and accessibility.
